Weekly print schedule

Takes your batch, works out the plates needed for one week, and lays them across your printers — with the changeover gap between plates and only starting inside each printer's daily hours.

These numbers cover materials, equipment, running costs and printer time only. They do not put a value on your own labour — modelling, slicing, post-processing, packing, listing and customer messages all come out of your time on top.
